Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an event?

On the home screen, tap the "Create Event" button at the bottom of the list. Enter an event name and add at least 2 members.

How do I add a payment?

Open an event, then tap "Add Payment" in the Payments section. Select who paid, enter the amount, describe what it was for, and choose who should split the cost.

How is the settlement calculated?

The app calculates the minimum number of transfers needed to settle all debts. For example, if 3 people each owe different amounts, the app finds the most efficient way to balance everything.

Can I split a payment among specific people only?

Yes! When adding a payment, use the "TO (SPLIT BETWEEN)" section to select specific members. By default, all members are selected.

How do I change my currency?

Tap the gear icon on the top right of the home screen to open Settings. Tap your current country to change the currency.

Is my data safe?

All data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. If you delete the app, all data is permanently removed.

How do I share the settlement?

In the Settlement section of an event, tap "Share Settlement". This generates a summary image and opens your device's share sheet so you can send it via any messaging app.
